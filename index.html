TUTSAK — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

TUTSAK is a multiplayer horror game. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how the game handles your data.

In short: TUTSAK does not collect or store any personal data. Camera and microphone data are never recorded or shared with third parties. Voice chat audio is transmitted in real time only to other players in your session and is never stored.

Camera Feature (Optional)

TUTSAK includes an optional "Blink Detection" feature that uses your device's camera to detect eye blinks for gameplay purposes.

How it works:

The camera feed is processed entirely on your local device. The only data produced is a simple true/false value indicating whether your eyes are open or closed. This value is used solely to control in-game mechanics.

What we do NOT do:

We do not record, store, or save any images or video from your camera. We do not transmit any camera data to any server, cloud service, or third party. We do not use facial recognition or store any biometric data. We do not use the camera for any purpose other than blink detection gameplay.

Your control:

The blink detection feature is disabled by default. You must manually enable it in the game settings. You can disable it at any time. When disabled or when the game is closed, camera access is completely terminated.

Voice Chat (Optional)

TUTSAK includes optional proximity voice chat so you can talk to other players during a session.

How it works:

Your microphone is muted by default. When you enable it, your voice is transmitted in real time through Photon's voice servers to the other players in your game session only. During a session, recent voice audio may be temporarily processed in your device's memory to power in-game effects (for example, game characters may react to or imitate sounds heard during the session). This processing happens entirely on each player's device, covers only the last few minutes of the current session, and is never written to disk.

What we do NOT do:

We do not record or save voice audio to any file. We do not store voice data after the game session ends. We do not transmit voice audio to anyone outside your game session. We do not analyze voice data or use it for any purpose other than in-game features.

Your control:

Voice chat is opt-in — your microphone stays off until you enable it, and you can mute it at any time. Closing the game or leaving a session immediately discards all temporarily held audio.

Multiplayer

TUTSAK uses Photon Networking for multiplayer functionality. When playing online, standard networking data (such as player position and game state) is transmitted between players through Photon's servers. Voice chat audio is also relayed through Photon's servers and is not stored by us. Please refer to Photon's Privacy Policy for details on their data handling practices.

Data Collection

TUTSAK itself does not collect any personal information, analytics, telemetry, or usage data. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any other tracking technology.

Third-Party Services

The game is distributed through Steam. Please refer to Steam's Privacy Policy for information about data collected by the Steam platform.

Children's Privacy

TUTSAK is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
